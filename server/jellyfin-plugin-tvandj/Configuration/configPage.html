TV and J

Household settings shared by the TV and J apps on every device and for every user. Change them from the apps' Settings as an administrator.


                


                
Scrobbling

                
Everyone connects their own accounts in TV and J's Settings, and what they play in any Jellyfin app is scrobbled: music to Last.fm and ListenBrainz, movies and shows to Trakt. ListenBrainz works as is. Last.fm and Trakt need this server registered with them once: create the app, then paste its credentials here.

                
If the separate Last.fm, ListenBrainz or Trakt plugins are installed too, turn off scrobbling in one or the other, or plays count twice.

                

                    
Last.fm

                    
Create an API account at last.fm/api/account/create; the callback URL can stay empty.

                    

                    

                    
Trakt

                    
Create an app at trakt.tv/oauth/applications/new with the redirect URI urn:ietf:wg:oauth:2.0:oob.
